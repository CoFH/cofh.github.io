---
title: Distribution types
---

This page lists the available distribution types that can be used to distribute
features generated using [CoFH World](../../) in the world. The distribution
type to use is specified when describing a [feature](../feature-format/).

<div class="uk-overflow-container">
  <table class="uk-table uk-table-striped uk-table-condensed uk-text-small cofh-table-semi-compress">
    <thead>
      <tr>
        <th>Distribution type</th>
        <th>Description</th>
      </tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr>
        <td>[`uniform`](../distribution-types/uniform/)</td>
        <td>Features are evenly distributed between a mininum and maximum altitude.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`gaussian`](../distribution-types/gaussian/)</td>
        <td>Features are distributed spread around a certain altitude.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`surface`](../distribution-types/surface/)</td>
        <td>Features are distributed on the surface.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`fractal`](../distribution-types/fractal/)</td>
        <td>Features are distributed together in box-shaped areas.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`underwater`](../distribution-types/underwater/)</td>
        <td>Features are distributed at the bottom of bodies of water.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`underfluid`](../distribution-types/underfluid/)</td>
        <td>Features are distributed at the bottom of bodies of certain types of fluid.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`cave`](../distribution-types/cave/)</td>
        <td>Features are distributed in the floors or ceilings of caves.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`sequential`](../distribution-types/sequential/)</td>
        <td>Features are distributed multiple times in the same chunk, using one distribution after
          the other.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`custom`](../distribution-types/custom/)</td>
        <td>Features are distributed at user-specified coordinates in each chunk.</td>
      </tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</div>
