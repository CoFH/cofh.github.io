---
title: Feature types
---

This page lists the available types of features that can be generated in the
world using [CoFH World](../../). The feature types to use are specified when
describing [features](../feature-format/).

<div class="uk-overflow-container">
  <table class="uk-table uk-table-striped uk-table-condensed uk-text-small cofh-table-semi-compress">
    <thead>
      <tr>
        <th>Feature type</th>
        <th>Description</th>
      </tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr>
        <td>[`cluster`](../feature-types/cluster/)</td>
        <td>A somewhat round deposit of blocks; often used for generating ores.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`sparse-cluster`](../feature-types/sparse-cluster/)</td>
        <td>Like `cluster`, but generates more rarely with smaller sizes.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`large-vein`](../feature-types/large-vein/)</td>
        <td>A deposit consisting of a group of branches that start from a center point; can get very
          large.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`decoration`](../feature-types/decoration/)</td>
        <td>A group of individual blocks scattered around on a surface.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`lake`](../feature-types/lake/)</td>
        <td>Shaped like naturally generated ponds of water or lava.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`plate`](../feature-types/plate/)</td>
        <td>A vertical cylinder of blocks, similar to clay deposits.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`geode`](../feature-types/geode/)</td>
        <td>Shape is similar to `lake`, except completely filled with and surrounded by blocks.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`spike`](../feature-types/spike/)</td>
        <td>An upwards pointing spike, like the ones in ice plains spikes biomes.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`boulder`](../feature-types/boulder/)</td>
        <td>A group of nearly spherical deposits of blocks.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`stalagmite`](../feature-types/stalagmite/)</td>
        <td>An upwards pointing spike.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`stalactite`](../feature-types/stalactite/)</td>
        <td>A downwards pointing spike.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`small-tree`](../feature-types/small-tree/)</td>
        <td>A small oak-shaped tree.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`spout`](../feature-types/spout/)</td>
        <td>A column of blocks of a certain height, generated from the bottom up.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`structure`](../feature-types/structure/)</td>
        <td>A structure loaded from an NBT file.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`sequential`](../feature-types/sequential/)</td>
        <td>Multiple features at the same location, one after the other.</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>[`consecutive`](../feature-types/consecutive/)</td>
        <td>Loops through a list of features, generating one at a time.</td>
      </tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</div>
