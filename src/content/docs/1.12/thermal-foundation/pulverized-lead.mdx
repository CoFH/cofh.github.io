**Pulverized lead** is a raw material. It is the dust form of
[lead](../lead-ingot/).


Obtaining
---------

### Pulverizer
<RecipeTable
  recipes={[
    tePulverizer.pulverizedLead,

  ]}
  noResult
/>

### Crafting
<RecipeTable recipes={[tfCrafting.petrotheum.leadIngot, tfCrafting.petrotheum.leadOre]} noResult />

### Centrifugal Separator
<RecipeTable recipes={[teCentrifuge.enderiumBlend]} noResult />

### Smashing
When [lead ore](../lead-ore/) is broken using a
[Smashing](../../cofh-core/smashing/) enchanted
[pickaxe](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pickaxe) or similar tool, two piles of
pulverized lead are dropped instead of the ore.
