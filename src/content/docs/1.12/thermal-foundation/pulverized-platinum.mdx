**Pulverized platinum** is a raw material. It is the dust form of
[platinum](../platinum-ingot/).


Obtaining
---------

### Pulverizer
<RecipeTable
  recipes={[
    tePulverizer.pulverizedPlatinum,

    tePulverizer.oreProcessing.iridium,
  ]}
  noResult
/>

<RecipeTable

  ]}

### Centrifugal Separator
<RecipeTable recipes={[teCentrifuge.enderiumBlend]} noResult />

### Smashing
When [platinum ore](../platinum-ore/) is broken using a
[Smashing](../../cofh-core/smashing/) enchanted
[pickaxe](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pickaxe) or similar tool, two piles of
pulverized platinum are dropped instead of the ore.
