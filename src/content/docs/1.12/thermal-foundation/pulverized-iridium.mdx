**Pulverized iridium** is a raw material. It is the dust form of
[iridium](../iridium-ingot/).


Obtaining
---------

### Pulverizer
<RecipeTable
  recipes={[
    tePulverizer.pulverizedIridium,

    tePulverizer.oreProcessing.platinum,
  ]}
  noResult
/>

### Crafting
<RecipeTable
  recipes={[
    tfCrafting.petrotheum.iridiumIngot,
    tfCrafting.petrotheum.iridiumOre,
  ]}
  noResult
/>

### Smashing
When [iridium ore](../iridium-ore/) is broken using a
[Smashing](../../cofh-core/smashing/) enchanted
[pickaxe](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pickaxe) or similar tool, two piles of
pulverized iridium are dropped instead of the ore.
