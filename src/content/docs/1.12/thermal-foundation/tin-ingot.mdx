Trivia
------

* If [Tinkers'
  Construct](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/tinkers-construct) is
  installed, molten tin can be mixed with molten [silver](../silver-ingot/)
  and [energized glowstone](../energized-glowstone/) to make molten
  [lumium](../lumium-ingot/).
