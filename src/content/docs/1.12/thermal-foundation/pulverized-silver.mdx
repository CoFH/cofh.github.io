[silver](../silver-ingot/).

### Smashing
When [silver ore](../silver-ore/) is broken using a
[Smashing](../../cofh-core/smashing/) enchanted
[pickaxe](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pickaxe) or similar tool, two piles of
pulverized silver are dropped instead of the ore.
