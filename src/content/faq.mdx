## Releases

### Which Minecraft versions are supported?

See the [Mods](/mods/) page for the latest supported Minecraft version. Older versions get no new
content or bug fixes.

### Is there an ETA for X?

No. Things come out as they are ready. Please be patient and understanding.

### Will you make Fabric, Quilt or Bedrock versions?

No. We have no plans to put our mods on Fabric, Quilt or Bedrock. If you want to play Fabric mods
alongside ours, [Sinytra Connector](https://modrinth.com/mod/connector) runs many of them on Forge
and NeoForge.

### Can I port a CoFH mod to another loader?

No. CoFH mods cannot be ported to other loaders. This includes Thaumcraft.

### What are the plans for Thaumcraft?

It is being worked on; there is no ETA and there are no early builds. Join our
[Discord](https://discord.gg/uRKrnbH) for teasers and development updates.

## Using the mods

### Can I use your mods in my modpack?

Yes.

### Where are the configs?

Some configs are in the normal `config` folder; others are in the `serverconfig` folder inside your
world folder. To apply those to every new world, copy the changed files into the `defaultconfigs`
folder.

### Where are the docs for newer versions?

They are being worked on. Until then, use the Thermalpedia
([Patchouli](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/patchouli)) and
[JEI](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jei). The existing
[Minecraft 1.12 docs](/mods/1.12/) will remain available.

### How do I add recipes with KubeJS or CraftTweaker?

Ask on their Discord servers: you will get faster and better help there. We are happy to help with
data packs.

### Which mods are known to cause problems?

OptiFine crashes the game. Remove it.

### Where can I get help, report bugs or suggest things?

On [Discord](https://discord.gg/uRKrnbH): `#bug-reports` for bugs, `#suggestions` for ideas and
`#tech-support` for help.

## Thermal Series

### My game asks for "thermal". Which mod is that?

That is [Thermal Foundation](/mods/#thermal-foundation). It's required when installing any other
mod in the Thermal Series.

### Where are the tools made from Thermal metals?

They moved to [Tool's Complement](/mods/#tools-complement). It doesn't need Thermal: tools appear
for any mod that adds the metals.

### Where are tiered ducts, itemducts and covers?

Tiered ducts are gone: ducts move plenty without upgrades. Itemducts will return later.
Duct covers are not planned for now.

### Why is my dynamo slowly losing power?

A dynamo's energy buffer is only a throttle; dynamos are not meant to store energy. Use a Redstone
Flux Cell for that.

### Can you make mod X work with Thermal machines?

Sure, as long as it doesn't break the other mod or conflict with Thermal. Post it in `#suggestions`
on [Discord](https://discord.gg/uRKrnbH).

### How do I change ore generation in the newer versions?

There is a config to turn the ores on or off. To change sizes, counts and heights, use a
[data pack](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Data_pack) that overrides
`data/thermal/worldgen/placed_feature`. Copy those files from the Thermal Foundation jar or
[its GitHub repository](https://github.com/CoFH/ThermalFoundation/tree/1.20.1/src/main/generated/data/thermal/worldgen/placed_feature)
and change `size`, `count`, `min_inclusive` or `max_inclusive`. You can use
[Open Loader](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/open-loader) to apply the data pack to
every new world.

### Is the Blitz being removed now that the Breeze exists?

No. The Breeze is air; the Blitz is lightning.

## Units and terms

### What is Redstone Flux (RF)?

The energy system of Team CoFH's mods. It is the same as Forge Energy (FE), so Thermal machines
work with any mod that uses it.

See [Redstone Flux: A Primer](/posts/2014/10/10/redstone-flux-a-primer/) for an in-universe
explanation.

### What is the history of Redstone Flux?

Thermal Expansion originally ran on BuildCraft's Minecraft Joules (MJ). As MJ's API grew
increasingly complex and opinionated, Thermal Expansion 3 for Minecraft 1.6 replaced it with
Redstone Flux. RF was simpler and lossless, and any mod could include it without depending on
Thermal Expansion.

Many other mods quickly adopted Redstone Flux. Eventually, Forge based its own energy system, Forge
Energy, on it. Our mods switched to Forge Energy too, but still call it RF.

### What is a millibucket (mB)?

The unit for amounts of [fluid](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fluid): one bucket holds 1,000 mB. It is
the unit of the fluid system in Forge and NeoForge, which lets mods move and store fluids in amounts
smaller than a bucket.

### What do RF/t and mB/t mean?

RF or mB per [tick](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Tick) (1/20 of a second): the rate at which energy or
fluid is moved, produced or used.

### What is the difference between RF and RF/t?

RF is an amount of energy; RF/t is a rate, also called power. A job takes a fixed amount of energy,
and the machine's power decides how long it takes. For example, a Pulverizer spends 4,000 RF per
ore. At 20 RF/t that takes 200 ticks (10 seconds), and upgrading its maximum power usage shortens
that time.

## Team CoFH

### What does CoFH stand for?

<dfn>Cult of the Full Hub</dfn>: the name of King Lemming and Cynycal's LAN gaming group in the late
'90s, also known as a hub. Its goal was to fill the hub to its cap of 8 people, and eventually it
did so routinely.

### What are the origins of Team CoFH?

In 2012, King Lemming and Cynycal teamed up as Team CoFH to make Minecraft mods. It was mostly a
way for King Lemming to learn Java. They started on Brewcraft, a farming mod with a brewing
overhaul.

Soon after, King Lemming began experimenting with a furnace that ran on BuildCraft's Minecraft
Joules. More blocks like it followed, and together they became the first version of
[Thermal Expansion](/mods/#thermal-expansion), for Minecraft 1.2. Brewcraft was left behind.

Since then, Thermal Expansion grew and eventually split up into the Thermal Series. One of its mods,
[Thermal Cultivation](/mods/#thermal-cultivation), is a farming mod, bringing things full circle.

### Who is part of Team CoFH today?

It changes over time, as people join, move on or help with a single mod. King Lemming still leads
development. You can find the current team on [GitHub](https://github.com/CoFH) and on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/uRKrnbH).
